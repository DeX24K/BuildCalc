Measure your roof footprint, choose your slope, and get the exact number of squares, bundles of shingles and rolls of underlayment.

Roof footprint

How it works: footprint area × pitch factor = roof area. 1 square = 100 sq ft. 3 bundles per square. Add 10% waste. Underlayment: 1 roll covers 4 squares.

How many bundles of shingles do I need?

Multiply your roof's footprint (length × width) by the pitch factor for the slope. Divide by 100 for squares, multiply by 3 bundles per square, and add ~10% for waste, starter and ridge cap. A 40×25 roof at 4/12 pitch needs about 33 squares or **99 bundles** plus waste.

Tip: buy all shingles at once from the same production run so the color matches.

Frequently asked questions

How many bundles of shingles do I need?

3 bundles per square (100 sq ft), plus 10% extra for waste and starters.

How do I measure my roof area?

Measure the footprint and multiply by the pitch factor — 1.06 for 4/12, 1.12 for 6/12, 1.20 for 8/12, 1.28 for 10/12.
