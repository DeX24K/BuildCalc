Wall size

How it works: a modular brick with mortar joints covers ~135 sq in, so you need about 7 bricks per sq ft. Add 10% for breakage. Mortar: 1 bag per 30 bricks.

How many bricks do I need?

Multiply wall length by height for the square footage, then multiply by 7 (bricks per square foot for modular brick with mortar joint). Add 10% for breakage and odd cuts. A 10×6 wall needs about 60 sq ft × 7 = 420 bricks, plus 42 extra = ~462 bricks.

Mortar tip: one bag of premixed mortar covers roughly 30 bricks.

Frequently asked questions

How many bricks do I need per square foot?

About 7 for modular brick with a 3/8" mortar joint.

How much mortar do I need?

One 60-80 lb bag covers about 30-35 bricks.
