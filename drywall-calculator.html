Drywall Calculator

Find out how many sheets of drywall, screws and joint compound you need for walls and ceilings.

Room size

How it works: wall area = perimeter × height, plus the ceiling. Divide by sheet coverage (48 sq ft for 4×12). 1 screw per sq ft, 4.5 lb of compound per 100 sq ft.

How much drywall do I need?

Calculate total surface: walls (perimeter × height) + ceiling (length × width). A 10×10 room with 8' ceilings has about 480 sq ft — 10 sheets of 4×12 drywall. Buy screws at ~1 per square foot and compound at ~4.5 lb per 100 sq ft.

Tip: use 4×12 sheets vertically to cut fewer seams on 8-foot walls.
