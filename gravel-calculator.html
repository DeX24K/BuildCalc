Gravel Calculator

Find out how many cubic yards and tons of gravel you need for driveways, walkways, patios and flower beds.

Area to cover

How it works: volume = length × width × (depth ÷ 12) → cubic feet. Divide by 27 for yards. Multiply yards by 1.4 for tons.

How much gravel do I need?

Measure the length, width and desired depth in feet. Multiply for cubic feet, divide by 27 for cubic yards. Most gravel weighs about 1.4 tons per cubic yard, so multiply yards by 1.4 for tons. A 20×20 driveway at 4" deep needs roughly 4.9 yards — about 6.9 tons.

Global tip: order in yards. Suppliers price per yard, and it's the unit you'll see on the delivery ticket.

Frequently asked questions

How many tons is a cubic yard of gravel?

About 1.4 tons for typical crushed stone and pea gravel. Larger rock weighs slightly less per yard.

What depth do I need?

2-3 inches for walkways, 4-5 inches for driveways over compacted ground, up to 12 inches for a gravel paver base.
