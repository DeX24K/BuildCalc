Room measurements

How it works: wall area = perimeter × height. Divide by the usable coverage per double roll. Subtract windows/doors if you measured them out. Round up.

How many rolls of wallpaper do I need?

Add all wall lengths together for the perimeter, multiply by wall height, and divide by the roll's usable coverage. Standard double rolls cover about 56 sq ft with a small pattern repeat. Large repeats waste more per strip — use the 42 sq ft figure to be safe.

Quick rule of thumb: a 12×12 room (48 ft perimeter, 8 ft walls) needs about 8 double rolls.

Frequently asked questions

How many rolls of wallpaper do I need for a 12×12 room?

About 8 double rolls for 384 sq ft of wall with a small repeat.

Should I buy extra wallpaper?

Yes — buy 1-2 extra rolls so color lots match for future repairs.
