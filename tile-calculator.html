Area to cover

How it works: area = length × width, plus 10% for cuts and breakage. Then we divide by the coverage of each tile and round up to whole tiles (boxes of 10).

How many boxes of tile do I need?

Measure the room length and width in feet. Multiply them for the square footage, then add 10% (the standard breakage and cut allowance). Each box of 10 tiles covers about 11 sq ft for 12×12 tiles. Divide your total by 11 and round up.

Quick rule of thumb: a 10×10 room needs about 110 sq ft of tile — around 10 boxes of 12×12 tiles.

Frequently asked questions

How much extra tile should I buy?

Buy 10% extra for straight layouts and 15–20% if installing diagonally, because diagonal cuts create much more waste.

Should I buy extra tile for future repairs?

Yes. Buying one extra box is cheap insurance — the same color and dye lot often isn't available years later.
