Room dimensions

How it works: wall area = perimeter × height − (door + window areas). One gallon covers ~350 sq ft. 10% extra for waste and touch-ups. Pages assume an 8ft ceiling, 21 sq ft per door, 15 sq ft per window.

How much paint do I need?

Most paint cans cover 350–400 square feet per gallon. To find out how much paint you need:

  • Measure the length and width of the room.
  • Calculate the wall area: 2 × (length + width) × ceiling height.
  • Subtract doors (~21 sq ft each) and windows (~15 sq ft each).
  • Add the ceiling if you're painting it.
  • Multiply by the number of coats, then add 10% for waste.

Quick rule of thumb: a standard 12×12 bedroom needs about 2 gallons of paint for one coat on walls and ceiling, or 4 gallons for two coats.

How many coats do I need?

Two coats give the best coverage and durability, especially when painting over a darker color. Our calculator defaults to 2 coats since that's what most interior jobs need.

Should I paint the ceiling?

If the ceiling is the same color as the walls or white, include it. A 12×12 ceiling is roughly 144 sq ft, which is about half a gallon of paint per coat.

Frequently asked questions

How much paint do I need for a 12×12 room?

A typical 12×12 bedroom with 8-foot ceilings needs about 2 gallons for one coat on walls and ceiling (4 gallons for two coats).

How many square feet does a gallon of paint cover?

One gallon covers roughly 350–400 sq ft with one coat. We use 350 sq ft to be safe.

Does paint type change the amount?

Yes. Primer and flat paint cover slightly better; high-gloss and textured paints may need more. Add 10% if unsure.
