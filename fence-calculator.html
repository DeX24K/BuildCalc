Fence Calculator

Find out how many panels, posts and bags of concrete you need for a fence line, based on standard 8-foot panels.

Fence line

How it works: panels = length ÷ panel width (rounded up). Posts = panels + 1 (add corners/gates separately). 1 bag of 80 lb concrete per post.

How many fence panels do I need?

Divide the total fence length by the panel width. A 100-foot fence with 8-foot panels needs 13 panels and 14 posts. Corner posts, gate posts and end posts may need extra concrete — use 1.5 bags there.

Concrete tip: an 80 lb bag per post holds better than quick-setting alternatives for corner posts.

Frequently asked questions

How many fence panels do I need?

Length ÷ panel width, rounded up. A 100-ft fence = 13 panels (8 ft).

How much concrete per fence post?

One 80 lb bag per post; one and a half for corners and gates.
