Flooring Calculator

Find out how much laminate, hardwood or vinyl flooring you need in square feet and boxes, including the correct waste allowance for your layout.

Room size

How it works: area = length × width. Multiply by your waste factor. A box of laminate typically covers 20 sq ft, so we divide and round up to whole boxes.

How much flooring do I need?

Measure the room in feet, multiply length by width for the area, and add waste. Straight installations need 5–10%, while diagonal or herringbone patterns need 10–15% because cutting angles creates more offcuts. Most laminate boxes cover about 20 sq ft.

Quick rule of thumb: a 12×12 room needs ~158 sq ft with 10% waste — around 8 boxes of laminate.

Frequently asked questions

How much flooring do I need for a 12×12 room?

144 sq ft, plus ~14 sq ft of waste = about 158 sq ft, or 8 boxes of laminate.

Do underlayment and padding add to the area?

Buy the same square footage of underlayment as your flooring. Buy a little extra to overlap seams by 2 inches.
